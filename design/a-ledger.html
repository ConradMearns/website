I'm Conrad Mearns, a Computer Scientist, Technologist, Technician, builder, measurer, yearner. Here you'll find quick links to some of my favorite things on the internet — old rants, ramblings, mutterings, and utterances I've long forgotten about — experiments, toys, projects, and some multimedia may wind up here too.
