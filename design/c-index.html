Hello! I'm Conrad Mearns, a Computer Scientist, Technologist, Technician, builder, measurer, yearner. Here you'll find quick links to some of my favorite things on the internet — old rants, ramblings, mutterings, and utterances I've long forgotten about — experiments, toys, projects, and some multimedia may wind up here too.

Who knows how many times I've rewritten this website. I usually have a lofty vision and a free weekend, and get only as far as my work-burnout allows. For this reason I'm trying yet again to take a step back: make something kinda pretty, make something easy for myself to maintain.
