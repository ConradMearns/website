Design principles, and where they collide

A linked reference to the hard-to-define rules of software design. Each entry gives the primary source, the claim it makes, the strength of the evidence, and the principles it fights with.

Most of these principles answer the same three questions: where knowledge lives, how far a change spreads, and how quickly you find out you were wrong. The interesting part is where they disagree, and the tensions section sets those conflicts side by side.

      Ethics, as its own discussion

      This is not a sixth theme. The five themes are about what makes software good to build and maintain, and a coding standard can take a position on each. Ethics asks who bears the cost when the software runs. That is a separate discussion with its own literature, so it is kept apart here. Where a principle above touches it, the entries link across.
