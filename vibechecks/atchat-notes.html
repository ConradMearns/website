atchat

A chat with no chat server. Your browser logs in to Bluesky with an app password, swaps connection offers and answers through records in your own account, then talks to your contact directly: messages, photos that live for six seconds, and video clips up to eight.

Use a Bluesky app password, never your account password. It is one HTML file and only talks to Bluesky.
