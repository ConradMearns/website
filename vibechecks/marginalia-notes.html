Marginalia

v1

A qualitative coder that slides a window over your text, embeds each window, and scores it against a codebook. It runs in the tab, and you bring your own OpenRouter key.

v2

The second try: each code is a matched filter over token embeddings, with positive examples that make it fire and negative examples that subtract look-alikes. All in the browser.
