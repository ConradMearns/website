Endless Tupo

An unofficial endless version of Tupo by Josh Collinsworth. Fill a 4×4 or 5×5 board so each row and column holds every number and every color once, and every number + color pair appears once on the board. A ledger tracks which pairs are placed, and each difficulty level needs one more kind of check. Hint describes the next deduction and highlights the tiles it relies on.
